## Recommended project framing for judges and stakeholders

This project is best presented as:

- an explainable AI + molecular simulation prototype
- a decision-support platform for structural resilience screening
- a deep-space protein stress-testing workflow
- a surrogate-model demo for biological nanomachine viability assessment

## Important scientific framing

The project should be pitched as a radiation-inspired proxy and structural resilience workflow rather than a direct physical radiation-transport simulation.

This keeps the claims honest while still highlighting the technical value.

## Suggested repo homepage text

COSMORA explores whether selected protein domains could serve as nanoscale mechanical components in deep-space environments. It combines short OpenMM simulations, structural analysis, and explainable ML to estimate resilience-related risk under controlled proxy conditions.
